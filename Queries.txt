10) Find out the experience (year) of employees and display empname,hiredate and experience in year.
	select ename,hiredate,floor((months_between(sysdate,hiredate))/12) 	"Experience" from emp4;
	
	
11) Increase the salary 10% of employees whose experience is grater than 43.

	update emp4
	set sal=sal+sal*0.1
	where floor((months_between(sysdate,hiredate))/12) > 43;
